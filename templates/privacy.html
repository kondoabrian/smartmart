SMARTMART INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This page explains how SmartMart handles information provided by customers when using the website.

01

Introduction

SmartMart respects the privacy of its customers and aims to handle customer information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use the SmartMart website and place an order.

02

Information We Collect

When you place an order, SmartMart may collect information required to process and deliver your order, including:

  • ✓ Customer name
  • ✓ Phone number
  • ✓ District
  • ✓ Town
  • ✓ Delivery address
  • ✓ Additional delivery directions
  • ✓ Order and product information
03

How We Use Information

Customer information may be used to:

  • ✓ Process customer orders
  • ✓ Prepare products for delivery
  • ✓ Contact customers about their orders
  • ✓ Arrange and manage deliveries
  • ✓ Provide order tracking
  • ✓ Respond to customer enquiries
04

Order Information

Information associated with an order may be stored in SmartMart's system so that the order can be processed, managed and tracked.

05

WhatsApp Communication

SmartMart may use WhatsApp as a communication channel for customer orders and enquiries. When you choose to communicate through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

06

Sharing Customer Information

Customer information should only be accessed by people who need it to perform relevant SmartMart operations, such as processing an order or arranging delivery.

07

Information Security

SmartMart takes reasonable steps to protect customer information and limit unauthorized access. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

08

Customer Responsibility

Customers should provide accurate information when placing an order, particularly their phone number and delivery details, so that SmartMart can communicate with them and deliver their order correctly.

09

Changes to This Policy

SmartMart may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the website, services or business operations.
